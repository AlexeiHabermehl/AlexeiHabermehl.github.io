Full toolkit

Corporate finance and valuation

Time value of money (annuities, loan amortization, retirement planning) · Capital budgeting with NPV, IRR, payback and profitability index, including when IRR breaks down · Cost of capital: CAPM, after-tax cost of debt, WACC, bottom-up beta · Ratio analysis and DuPont analysis of ROE · 3-statement and 5-year forecast models in Excel · DCF with exit-multiple or perpetuity-growth terminal value, plus sensitivity tables · Relative valuation with P/E, P/B, P/S and EV/EBITDA · M&A and LBO basics: acquisition premiums, comparable companies, enterprise-to-equity value, deal structuring (earn-outs, covenants, asset purchases) · Optimal capital structure, dividend and buyback policy

Investments

Risk and return, Sharpe ratio, mean–variance optimization with Excel Solver · Options: payoffs, straddles, covered calls, protective puts, put–call parity, binomial and Black–Scholes pricing · Futures and forwards: basis, cost of carry, hedge ratios · Performance measurement: dollar- vs. time-weighted returns, tracking error, attribution · Alternative investments (private equity, venture capital, hedge funds) and investment policy statements

Fixed income

Bond pricing, YTM, yield to call and yield to worst · Macaulay and modified duration, convexity · Duration gap analysis for bank interest-rate risk, applied to the SVB case · Callable bonds: embedded options, binomial interest-rate lattices, negative convexity · Credit analysis with S&P-style ratios, G-spread and Z-spread, secured vs. unsecured debt, Rule 144A offerings, make-whole calls · TIPS, century bonds, ESG and green bonds · Bond investment proposals and buy-side reports

International finance and hedging

Exchange-rate regimes and speculative attacks · Interest rate parity, forward rates, purchasing power parity · Transaction, translation and economic FX exposure · Financial vs. natural hedging, money-market hedges · Country-risk adjustments to cost of capital · Hedging FX and commodity exposure with forwards and futures

Financial markets and economics

The yield curve as a recession signal · Asset and credit bubbles, macroprudential policy · Central bank tools, inflation targeting vs. the dual mandate, bank capital, too-big-to-fail and moral hazard · Microeconomics: cost curves, breakeven and shutdown points, perfect competition, price elasticity

Operations and statistics

Little's Law, process analysis, bottlenecks, capacity and utilization · Queueing models and pooling · Linear programming with Solver · Newsvendor inventory decisions · Descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, one-way ANOVA, Levene's test, Tukey–Kramer · Databases, data warehouses and data marts; descriptive vs. predictive vs. prescriptive analytics

Strategy, management and law

PESTEL, SWOT, VRIO, Porter's Five Forces · Business vs. corporate strategy · Motivation and leadership theories · HR and labor basics · Marketing: segmentation, targeting, positioning, the 4Ps · Commerce Clause and anti-commandeering cases · Employment law case briefs · Business ethics case analysis

Tools

Advanced Excel (NPV/IRR functions, data tables, Solver, Goal Seek) · Bloomberg Terminal and Bloomberg Market Concepts certificate · Refinitiv and Mergent · R for financial analysis (credit scoring, panel regression, random forests) · Case writing, memos, analyst reports and presentations
