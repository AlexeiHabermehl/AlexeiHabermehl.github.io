Valued Agnico-Eagle Mines (AEM), a Canadian gold producer centered on its LaRonde mine, to judge whether its April 2002 share-price decline signaled deeper risk or a buying opportunity.

**Methods:** free-cash-flow DCF discounted at WACC (CAPM cost of equity), terminal value, enterprise-to-equity bridge, and real options valuation of unmined reserves.
