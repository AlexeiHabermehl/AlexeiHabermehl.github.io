Assessed whether Kohler's board undervalued minority shares in its 1998 recapitalization, and whether the company should settle or go to trial.

**Methods:** DCF (WACC with re-levered beta from comparable companies) and market-multiple valuation. Our range was $55,427 to $158,725 per share.

**Team:** Abdoulie Bah, Alexei Habermehl, Mustafa Abuelrish, Victory Ejim
