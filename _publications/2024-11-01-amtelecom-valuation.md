Evaluated the board's decision to break up Amtelecom Group Inc. and sell its communications business, comparing a strategic sale, a common-share IPO, and an income trust IPO.

**Methods:** industry key-success-factor and SWOT analysis; DCF valuation giving an implied share price of $8.72.

**Team:** Abdoulie Bah, Alexei Habermehl, Mustafa Abuelrish, Victory Ejim
