Spotify's suggested-songs feature doesn't account for track traits such as danceability, tempo, key and year of release. This memo looks at which of those traits go with higher popularity scores, and whether the relationship changes by genre.

**Data:** a dataset of ~600,000 Spotify tracks, filtered to songs released after 2012.

**Key findings:**
- More danceable tracks are more likely to be popular, especially in Hip-Hop and Pop.
- The effects of danceability, tempo and key on popularity depend on genre.
- Newer music tends to be more popular across all categories.
