Compiles crash statistics for District of Columbia roadways from 2020 to 2026 and evaluates whether automated safety cameras (ASCs) reduce crash frequency and severity.

**Methods:** summary statistics (day of week, time of day, ward, severity, driver characteristics), time series, and a difference-in-differences (DiD) design comparing corridors that received new cameras with comparison corridors, with a pre-trend check and a robustness check.

**Key findings:**
- Crashes are driven mainly by seasonal trends, dropping in number and injury severity during winter months.
- Automated safety cameras have no statistically significant effect on crash frequency or severity.
- Speed cameras issue ~48% fewer tickets after one year, yet crash frequency and severity in the same areas stay relatively stable.
