Analyzed Spring 2025 transaction data from Grubhub's OneView platform to identify top-performing venues and menu items, and the time patterns that can inform staffing and inventory decisions. Combined product-mix data with nutrition and weather data.

**Methods:** descriptive analysis (venue, item, day, month), time series, regression (nutrition, weather, day of week), forecasting.

**Team:** Hannah Girshaw, Alexei Habermehl, Arun Pasupathy
